# Tuinplanner

**English** · [Nederlands](README.nl.md) · [Deutsch](README.de.md) · [Français](README.fr.md) · [Español](README.es.md)

A friendly garden planner for Android that works without internet. It tells you when to sow, plant, prune and
harvest, reminds you, and keeps track of what grows in your garden and at home. No account, no ads: your data
stays on your phone.

## What it does

- **Today**: the tasks of the moment, such as sowing, planting, pruning, harvesting, watering and feeding. Tick them off with one tap, or postpone them.
- **Plant library**: 447 plants and 21 bulb crops with sowing, planting, pruning and harvest months, good and bad neighbours, photos, sources and growing tips per month.
- **My garden**: the plants you grow, per year and per bed, with harvests, photos and notes. Perennials return every year.
- **Garden design**: draw your garden with beds, see the year plan, which plants get along, and how much sun a bed gets.
- **Calendar**: what to do per month or per year, also for your houseplants.
- **Houseplants**: watering and feeding rhythm, place, problem log, photos and safety for pets.
- **Diary and photos**: notes and a photo album per plant, garden or bed.
- **Stock and own tasks**: keep track of seeds and supplies and make your own recurring tasks.
- **Reminders and weather**: local notifications, weather advice and widgets for your home screen.
- **Search** everything in one place and **back up** all your data, photos included.

Simple or advanced: switch with one button. Advanced mode adds many optional features that you can switch on or
off one by one in Settings.

## Install

1. Open the **[Releases](../../releases)** page and download the newest `tuinplanner-<version>.apk` on your phone.
2. Open the file. If Android asks, allow installing from this source.
3. Start Tuinplanner. That is all.

The app is signed for sideloading and is not in the Play Store.

## Updates

Open **More → Settings** in the app and check for updates. The app finds the newest release here, downloads
it and asks Android to install it. Nothing is installed without your confirmation.

## Privacy

- Everything works offline; your plants, photos and notes stay on your device.
- The app only uses the internet for the optional weather advice (Open-Meteo) and to check for a new version.
- There are no accounts, no advertising and no tracking.

## Sources and credits
